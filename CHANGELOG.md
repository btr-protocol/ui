# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Account modal, dead click: "Switch wallet to X" and a click on an account only asked the CONNECTED wallet's picker, which cannot hold another wallet's account, and a failure was swallowed by the app, so nothing happened. The modal now runs the new `activateAccount(target, { entries?, signal?, probeMs? })` before the app's `onSwitchAccount`.
- `activateAccount` (exported), step 1: the account is already the connected wallet's active one (live `eth_accounts`): done, nothing else is touched. Step 2: every other announced EIP-6963 wallet answers a silent `eth_accounts` in parallel (3 s each; a WalletConnect session is one provider and is not searched).
- `activateAccount`, choice: exactly ONE wallet exposing the account, announced under a unique rdns, is bound. Several expose it, an rdns is announced twice, or the candidates cannot be told apart: nothing is bound and the panel says to choose the wallet with "Add or switch wallet" (`ambiguous`). Never the first match. An authorised account that is not that wallet's active one opens its own picker (EIP-1193 has no silent "set active").
- `activateAccount`, nobody exposes it: with no other wallet the connected one's picker opens (multi-account wallets); with exactly one other wallet it gets `eth_requestAccounts`, then its picker (a wallet that throws on `wallet_requestPermissions` falls back to what `eth_requestAccounts` returned); with several, `ambiguous`. Requests only ever go out from the user's click.
- `activateAccount`, safety: nothing is bound if the wallet state changed meanwhile (disconnect, `accountsChanged` on the connected wallet, a newer click, abort; closing the modal aborts). After the bind `eth_accounts` is re-read on the new provider: its first account must be the clicked one, else the previous wallet is restored (`not-shared`). The result carries `undo()`, which the modal calls when the app's own step fails or throws (the previous wallet is back and a row says so). The app step never runs after the modal closed or a newer click.
- Result: `{ ok: true, entry, accounts, undo } | { ok: false, reason: 'rejected' | 'not-shared' | 'ambiguous' | 'failed' | 'cancelled', wallet? }`. New `walletGeneration()` and `adoptWallet()` (what binding and rollback use). `switchWalletAccount(p, target)` is unchanged: only the provider it is given, never another wallet.
- No memory of which wallet held which account: nothing new is stored. The header glyph is the connected wallet's mark only while that wallet exposes the address shown, else the generic wallet icon (it was the connected wallet's mark even beside another wallet's account).
- Rollback: `undo` (and a failed re-read) re-reads the old wallet's accounts instead of replaying a snapshot (no answer: disconnected), restores `LAST_WALLET_KEY` to exactly its previous value, and a failed app step undoes even after the modal was closed. A disconnect during the re-read is never reconnected. `adoptWallet` now resolves the binding's generation and takes `keep`.
- `activateAccount`, trust gate: a wallet other than the connected one is probed, bound or sent any request ONLY when it is in the recent list (`readRecent()`, written by the connect modal; shared rdns are never recorded). Any other announced provider gets no call at all; with no recent candidate the result is `ambiguous` and the modal points to "Add or switch wallet". New result `failed`: the re-read after binding got no answer, so the previous wallet is back.
- Messages as list items: the modal's wrong-account warning, error and hints are `Notice size="row"` (new size: an `li` with the list's edges, padding and divider, a tinted fill, no card, the action below the text) in a `ul` at the top of the account list, `role="alert"` on the text. The nested card was also a `.surface` inside the modal's, which lowered the control radius of its buttons.
- The two actions are plain kit `Button`s sm (primary + secondary), one line (`whitespace-nowrap`), the address kept whole; their accessible name is the visible label + ` (full address)`. Addresses in the notice and hint text never split across lines. `NoticeAction` itself is unchanged for other notices.
- `AccountList` takes `notices`. REMOVED: `WrongAccountNotice` (the Safe app did not use it) and `AccountNoticeRow` (never released). The disconnect icon is a kit `Button` (ghost, icon-only). Rows with nothing to do (the current account) are plain rows: no hover, pointer or tab stop (`WalletItemButton` `inert`, `aria-current`).
- COPY: `notExposed` reads "Not in this wallet: select to find it". New labels `hintRejected`, `hintNotShared`, `hintAmbiguous` (`{addr}`, `{wallet}` named once, `{action}` = the `connectOther` label) and `hintFailed`; an app that translates by key adds them.
- Tests: `test/account-switch.test.tsx` (fake providers: unique holder, rogue second claimant, duplicate rdns, disconnect and accountsChanged during a pending request, two clicks, a wallet that drops the account after the bind, undo, app step false / throwing / after close, rejection, hanging and throwing providers, WalletConnect, no stored address) and `test/account-panel.browser.test.tsx` (Chromium: list-item geometry, action height / radius / one line at 1440, 390, 360 and a 360 px French render, activation, header glyph, inert row, keyboard).

## 0.12.2
- SIZE CHANGE (touch): on `pointer: coarse` every tab in a strip (group and attached; xs, sm and md) is at least `--ctl-m` (44 px) tall and wide. An xs tab was 32 px tall there, so an xs TAB STRIP is now 46 px (44 plus the group's two 1 px borders, the same as sm and md) while a plain xs `ButtonGroup` stays 34 px. Why they differ: a standalone button keeps its 44 px hit area through its `::after`; a tab inside the scroller cannot (the scroller clips the pseudo, and inside a strip it is zeroed because it overflowed the scroller by 1 px), so the tab's own box carries the floor. The 0.12.0 "pixel-identical to a button group" statement holds for sm and md on touch and for every size on fine pointers; the one exception is xs on a coarse pointer. On a phone, `xs` now means type size and padding only. Desktop is unchanged (xs strip 26 px).
- Widths on touch (re-measured at 390 px): a 1-character tab ("$") goes from 22.7 to 44 px, so `$ | APR` at xs goes from 66.4 to 90.0 px in total; the 4-option range strip (`1D 1W 1M 1Y`, sm) goes from 176.5 to 180.5 px in total, its short tabs to 44 px each. Strips that scroll keep their widths; their height changes with the rest.
- CONSUMER ACTION: check xs tabs next to xs Buttons or Inputs on phones (they are now 46 px against 34 px), rows with a fixed height under 46 px, and short `equal` strips (each tab is now at least 44 px wide).
- Hover no longer reads as selected: an unselected tab in a group presentation filled `bg-primary` on hover, the selected fill. It now takes `--bg-3` and `--fg-0`, as attached tabs did, and both only where hover exists (`hover: hover`), so an attached tab no longer keeps a hover tint after a tap. A selected tab keeps its look. A disabled tab does not change on hover (its idle fill is pinned, group and attached).
- False start-edge fade: the centring step no longer runs, and `data-l` / `data-r` are not set, when a strip lacks 2 px or less of its content (a shrink-wrapped group whose container is 1 to 2 px short, which centring scrolled by 1 to 2 px and dimmed the first tab). Real overflow is unchanged.
- RTL: `data-l` / `data-r` are the start / end edges (scrollLeft is negative in RTL) and the fade mask mirrors with `:dir(rtl)`.

## 0.12.1
- Tabs: a group that fits no longer fades its last label. On touch (`pointer: coarse`) the 44 px hit-area `::after` of each tab counted as scrollable overflow of the tab scroller (two xs tabs, `$` and `APR`: scrollWidth 66 over clientWidth 64), so `data-r` was set and the edge fade masked the end of the strip. Inside a tab strip that pseudo now takes the tab's own box (`min-width` / `min-height` 0; the scroller clipped it already), which also ends a vertical scroll of about 6 px the xs strip had on touch. Real overflow is unchanged.
- SCOPE CHANGE: the strip rules (`flex: none; white-space: nowrap`, and the hit-area above) now name the strip itself (`.tabs-attached-bar > .btn-group`). In 0.12.0 they also matched a plain `ButtonGroup` placed inside an attached tab's panel: its buttons did not wrap. They now behave like any plain `ButtonGroup`, so such a group may wrap its text where it did not. Standalone `ButtonGroup`s are unchanged (same width and height).
- Test: `test/tabs.browser.test.tsx`, 2 short tabs at xs / sm / md, group and attached, touch and not; plus the panel `ButtonGroup` hit-area and a width / height comparison against a plain group on the same page.

## 0.12.0
- Tabs on ButtonGroup. `SegmentedControl` (the kit's tab selector) is now a `ButtonGroup` of `Button`s: height, line height, padding, radius, border and dividers are the group's, so a tab row and a button group in one toolbar are pixel-identical. The pill container and its own tab styles are gone. API unchanged for existing call sites.

- Overflow: one line that scrolls, the edge fade as before; the active tab is centred from its measured rect (the scroll container is now positioned).
- CONSUMER ACTION: `SegmentedControl` requires a name for its tablist, `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` (a TypeScript error without one; the kit has no internal call site). Safe app sites to label: ActionsTabs:19, ActionsPage:68, ActivityFeed:118, DefiParams:110, HaltSheet:84, HoldingsChart:185 and :188, Params:182, SafeDetail:47, SendParams:157, VaultPage:147. The front keeps its own fork and is unaffected until it switches.
- ButtonGroup: dividers use `border-inline-start` (RTL). Plain groups wrap their text exactly as before; only tabs are `nowrap`.
- Tests: `test/tabs.browser.test.tsx` renders in Chromium (playwright-core, a new devDependency; fails with the install command, `bunx playwright install chromium`, where no Chromium is found).
